\# 📊 DataPulse — Business Intelligence \& Analytics Dashboard



A comprehensive \*\*Power BI Business Intelligence project\*\* designed to transform raw sales data into actionable business insights through data validation, SQL analytics, KPI analysis, customer intelligence, product performance, regional analysis, monthly trends, and anomaly detection.



\---



\## 🚀 Project Overview



\*\*DataPulse\*\* is a multi-page business intelligence dashboard built using \*\*Microsoft Power BI, SQL, Excel, and CSV data\*\*.



The project follows an end-to-end analytics workflow:



\*\*Raw Data → Data Validation → SQL Analytics → KPI Analysis → Business Analysis → Anomaly Intelligence → Power BI Dashboard → Business Insights\*\*



The dashboard provides decision-makers with a centralized view of sales performance and helps identify trends, high-performing segments, customer behavior, and unusual business activity.



\---



\## 🎯 Project Objectives



\* Analyze overall business performance

\* Track important sales KPIs

\* Evaluate regional and product performance

\* Analyze customer and order behavior
